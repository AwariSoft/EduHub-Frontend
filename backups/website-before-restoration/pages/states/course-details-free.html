  19. Course Description
  20. A good database starts with good questions. This practical introduction walks you through gathering requirements, identifying entities and choosing the relationships that keep your information consistent.
  21. You will design a small library database, turn an initial sketch into a normalized schema, and review common modeling mistakes. Short exercises help you practice each concept before moving on.
  22. Who is this course for?
  23. Students, aspiring analysts and developers who want a solid starting point in relational database design. No previous database experience is required.
  24. Before you begin
  25. Bring a notebook or diagramming tool and a real-world problem you would like to model.
